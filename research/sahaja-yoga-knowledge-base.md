## 1. What Sahaja Yoga is, in one breath

Sahaja Yoga is a meditation practice founded on **5 May 1970** by **Nirmala Srivastava (1923–2011)**, known as **Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi**.

- **It starts with an experience.** The first step is **Self-Realization**: the awakening of the **Kundalini**, an inner energy said to rest in the sacrum. The Kundalini rises through the chakras and emerges at the fontanel, felt as a **cool breeze** on the palms and above the head.
- **Then it is practised daily.** The goal of meditation is **thoughtless awareness** (*nirvichara samadhi*): fully alert, but with no unwanted thoughts. One description compares it to a lake without ripples.
- **It is always free.** No money is ever charged for self-realization or for classes, which volunteers run.
- **It is non-sectarian.** It is open to people of any religion, race or background.
- **The name explains the idea.** *Saha* means "with", *ja* means "born", so Sahaja means "born with you", and also "spontaneous". *Yoga* means "union" with the all-pervading power, not physical postures.

### Core vocabulary
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Kundalini** | "Coiled" (Sanskrit *kundala*). The maternal, conscious, evolutionary energy in the sacrum (*hieron osteon*, "sacred bone" in Greek). It is spoken of as "she". |
| **Self-Realization** | The Kundalini piercing the fontanel (*Brahmarandhra*) and connecting to the all-pervading power. It is described as "a true beginning", not the end point. |
| **Paramchaitanya** | The all-pervading power of divine love. Its flow is felt as **vibrations** (*chaitanya*): a cool breeze. |
| **Vibrations** | Breeze-like sensation on the hands and head. It can be cool (balanced) or warm, tingling or heavy (a "catch"). |
| **Catch** | A blockage felt as heat, tingling or heaviness on a specific finger, mapped to a specific chakra. |
| **Thoughtless awareness** | *Nirvichara*. Mental silence while fully alert, living in the present. |
| **Doubtless awareness** | *Nirvikalpa*. A later state of certainty about the divine. |
| **Subtle system** | 3 channels (nadis) + 7 main chakras + the Void + the Kundalini. |
| **Atma / Spirit** | The reflection of the divine in the heart; the true self. |
| **Bandhan** | A protective "shield" gesture made with the hand. |
| **Collective** | Group meditation and the community of practitioners. Meditating together is strongly encouraged, framed as cells in one body (*Virata*). |
| **Sahaja Yogi / Yogini** | A practitioner. |
| **Puja** | A devotional ceremony, mainly for practitioners. |

## 2. Founder: Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| **21 Mar 1923** | Born Nirmala Salve in **Chhindwara**, Madhya Pradesh, India. Her father, Prasad Rao Salve, was a scholar who helped write the Indian constitution (per the official site) and translated the Quran into Marathi. Her mother, Cornelia, is reported as the first woman in India with an honours degree in mathematics. The family was Christian; its forefathers converted out of protest at the treatment of Hindu widows. |
| From age 7 | Spent time at **Mahatma Gandhi's ashram**. Gandhi nicknamed her "Nepali". She told him: "Bapu, if you want to discipline people, why not give them discipline from within?" |
| 1942 | Youth leader in the **Quit India Movement** while at Science College, Nagpur. She was arrested and jailed. Her parents were jailed when she was 8, and she raised her siblings. |
| 1940s | Studied medicine at the Christian Medical College, Ludhiana, and Balakram Medical College, Lahore. |
| **7 Apr 1947** | Married **Chandrika Prasad Srivastava**. He later became Sir C.P. Srivastava (knighted 1990), was Secretary-General of the UN's International Maritime Organization (1974–89), and served under PM Lal Bahadur Shastri. |
| 1947–50 | Sheltered Muslim refugees during Partition. Daughters Kalpana (Dec 1947) and Sadhana (Feb 1950) were born. On 29 Jan 1948 Gandhi held Kalpana and asked, "When are you going to start your spiritual work?" He was assassinated the next day. |
| 1961 | Founded the **Youth Society for Films**; sat on the Film Censor Board. She was a music patron (Sur Singar Samsad, Music Club of Bombay). |
| **5 May 1970** | At **Nargol** (a beach town on India's west coast near Mumbai), she describes opening the **Sahasrara**. "The Kundalini rose like a telescope… like the colour of molten, red-hot iron… a big torrential rain of breeze started flowing through my head." This is the founding moment. The day is celebrated as **Sahasrara Day**. |
| 1970s | Began the work in Mumbai and then London, while her husband headed the IMO. |
| 1977–83 | Gave about 100 public talks at **Caxton Hall, London** (where Tagore lectured in 1913). Every evening ended with en-masse self-realization. Admission was free. |
| 1980s | Toured Europe, Australia and North America. Named "Personality of the Year" by the Italian Government (1986). |
| 1989 | First visit to the USSR. Sahaja Yoga received Soviet health-ministry sponsorship for research. |
| 1990s | South America, Eastern Europe, Asia, Pacific. Spoke at the UN for four consecutive years (1990–94). New York declared 26 Sep 1994 Shri Mataji Nirmala Devi Day. Spoke at the UN Women's Conference in Beijing (1995). Honorary doctorate in cognitive science from Bucharest (1995). World Philosophers Meet in Pune (1996). Claes Nobel tribute at the Royal Albert Hall (1997). |
| 2003 | Opened the **Vishwa Nirmala Prem Ashram** (Noida/Delhi, for destitute women and children) and the **P.K. Salve Kala Pratishthan** music academy (Nagpur, later Vaitarna). |
| 2006 | Honorary citizenship of **Cabella Ligure**, Italy. |
| 2009 | The raga **Nirmalkauns** was composed in her honour. |
| **23 Feb 2011** | Died in **Genoa, Italy**, aged 87. |
| 2023 | **Centenary.** "100 events in 100 countries", plus civic proclamations (e.g. Brampton, Canada) and events in Milan, Lucknow and Burlington. |

**Signature quotes** (as published on the official sites):
- "There can be no peace in the world until there is peace within."
- "You cannot know the meaning of your life until you are connected to the power that created you."
- "You have to be your own doctor. You have to be your own guru."
- "In Sahaja Yoga you don't have to do meditation. You are in meditation. You become meditative."
- "Like a wheel which is moving… if you jump on the axis of the wheel, it is silent."
- "You are an instrument most beautifully made… once you discover it, you will be amazed as to what you are."
- "Meditate so that you reach your inner being. This inner being is a vast ocean of bliss!"
- Images she used for the teaching: **"Like a candle which has been lit can light another candle"** (self-realization passed on freely), and **"meditation without self-realization is like trying to drive a car without starting the engine."**

**How she is presented:** public sites call her a founder and humanitarian, never a deity. Practitioners revere her as divine (the Adi Shakti / Holy Spirit). She claimed to be born realised and divine. **This gap between the public and the devotional view is the central tone decision for the site** (see §9).

## 3. The subtle system (the heart of any interactive site)

### 3.1 The three channels (nadis)
| Channel | Side / time | Governs | Pure quality | When out of balance | Cleared with |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ida** (left, moon) | Mooladhara up the left side to the right brain (**superego**). **Past.** | Emotions, desire, memory, conditioning | **Joy** | Depression, lethargy, guilt, low self-esteem | Candle flame, warm salt-water foot-soak |
| **Pingala** (right, sun) | Swadisthan up the right side to the left brain (**ego**). **Future.** | Action, planning, thinking | **Dynamism with a still mind** | Irritable, aggressive, over-thinking, stress; overheated **liver** ("seat of attention") | **Ice pack on the liver**, cool water |
| **Sushumna** (centre) | Sacrum to Sahasrara. **Present.** | Spiritual evolution, integration | Balance, peace | — | Meditation in thoughtless awareness |

The teaching: attention swings left to right, between past and future. Meditation settles it into the centre, the present. **This metaphor is the best one available for visual and interaction design.**

### 3.2 The chakras
*Petals and deities are practitioner knowledge; the public sites omit them. Everything else is from the official guides.*

| # | Chakra | Quality | Location | Colour | Element | Felt on | Petals · deity (practitioner) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mooladhara** | Innocence, wisdom | Pelvic plexus, base of spine | Coral red | Earth | Base of palm | 4 · Shri Ganesha |
| 2 | **Swadisthan** | Creativity, pure attention | Aortic plexus | Yellow | Fire | **Thumbs** | 6 · Brahmadeva-Saraswati |
| 3 | **Nabhi** | Generosity, contentment, evolution | Solar plexus, navel | Green | Water | **Middle fingers** | 10 · Vishnu-Lakshmi |
| 3a | **Void** (Bhavasagara) | Self-mastery, the "guru principle", dharma | Ring around the Nabhi | Green | Sky + salt water | Ring around palm centre | Adi Guru |
| 4 | **Anahat** (Heart) | Unconditional love, security, confidence | Cardiac plexus, behind the sternum | Purple-blue | Air | **Little fingers** | 12 · Jagadamba (left Shiva, right Rama) |
| 5 | **Vishuddhi** | Communication, diplomacy, collectivity | Cervical plexus, neck | Light blue | Ether | **Index fingers** | 16 · Radha-Krishna |
| 6 | **Agnya** | Forgiveness, humility, thoughtless awareness | Optic chiasma, forehead | Silver | Light | **Ring fingers** | 2 · Jesus Christ and Mary |
| 7 | **Sahasrara** | Integration, oneness, doubtless awareness | Limbic area, exits at the fontanel | All colours (rainbow lotus) | All | **Centre of palm** | 1000 · Adi Shakti |

**Hand map (official chart).** For both hands, palm up:
- Heel of the palm = 1 (Mooladhara)
- Thumb = 2 (Swadisthan)
- Index = 5 (Vishuddhi)
- Middle = 3 (Nabhi)
- Ring = 6 (Agnya)
- Little = 4 (Heart)
- Palm centre = 7 (Sahasrara), with the ring around it = 3a (Void)

Reading the hands:
- A sensation in the **left** hand means a left-channel catch; in the **right** hand, a right-channel catch; in **both**, the centre.
- To clear it, the **right hand** treats left or centre catches and the **left hand** treats right-side catches, pressing the chakra for 1–2 minutes.

> The hand map is the single most "interactive" piece of knowledge. It is literally a UI: a user taps the finger that tingles, and the app explains the chakra and the remedy.

### 3.3 Chakra details for content
- **Mooladhara**
  - "The support of the root." Innocence that "can never be destroyed, only covered by clouds".
  - Gives a sense of direction, memory and balance.
  - Remedy: sit on the earth, palms down.
- **Swadisthan**
  - Creativity and *attention* versus *thought*: "observe a flower without thinking about its name."
  - Supplies the brain. Over-thinking exhausts it and overloads the liver.
  - Remedies: ice for the right side, candle for the left.
- **Nabhi**
  - Satisfaction with material life (family, career, money) is the foundation from which seeking begins.
  - The liver is "vital to thoughtless awareness".
- **Void**
  - The "ocean of illusion", a gap in the central channel that the Kundalini bridges.
  - Self-discipline and becoming your own master. Salt water symbolises the guru principle.
- **Heart**
  - The spirit resides here. Unconditional love, confidence, absence of fear.
  - Affirmation: "I am one with my spirit."
- **Vishuddhi**
  - Diplomacy, humour, sweetness of speech, and the witness state.
  - Left side = guilt ("I am not guilty at all"); right side = harsh talk.
- **Agnya**
  - The narrow gate between ego (right) and superego (left), passable only through **forgiveness**.
  - Overexposure to screens weakens it.
- **Sahasrara**
  - The "thousand-petalled lotus" in the limbic area, where all chakras have their seat.
  - Felt as pulsing at the crown, then melting and coolness. It brings union of the individual soul with the universal (*atma* with *paramatma*).

## 4. The practice: exact procedures

### 4.1 Self-realization (the official 9 positions)
From shrimataji.org's illustrated guide. These positions are what the hero interaction should follow.
- **Setup:** sit comfortably, shoes off ("Mother Earth absorbs negativity"), left palm open upward on the lap (toward Shri Mataji's photo), eyes closed.
- **Hands:** the **right hand works on the left side of the body**.
- **The affirmations** are addressed to "Mother" (or "Shri Mataji"). The wording and counts below are the traditional guided wording. **Confirm them with a local Sahaja Yoga collective before shipping.**

| # | Position | Affirmation | Times |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Heart | "Mother, am I the Spirit?" | 3 |
| 2 | Upper left abdomen | "Mother, am I my own master?" | 3 |
| 3 | Lower left abdomen | "Mother, please give me pure knowledge." | 6 |
| 4 | Upper left abdomen | "Mother, I am my own master." | 10 |
| 5 | Heart | "Mother, I am not this body, not this mind, I am the pure Spirit." | 12 |
| 6 | Left neck/shoulder junction, head turned right | "Mother, I am not guilty at all." | 16 |
| 7 | Forehead, head bowed | "Mother, I forgive everyone." | From the heart |
| 8 | Back of head, head tilted back | "O Divine Power, if I have made any mistakes, please forgive me." | From the heart |
| 9 | Fontanel: palm down, fingers back, press and rotate clockwise | "Mother, please give me my self-realization." | 7 |

The counts follow the chakras' petal numbers (6 Swadisthan, 10 Nabhi, 12 Heart, 16 Vishuddhi), which is a nice detail for motion design.

**After:** feel for a cool or warm breeze with the left palm above the head, then the right. If nothing comes, say "I forgive everyone" a few more times. Warmth at first means the Kundalini is purifying; it cools with time.

### 4.2 Simplified first-week version (Sahaja Yoga USA, exact wording, secular)
1. Lower left side above the hip: **"Mother Kundalini, I want to experience the pure knowledge."**
2. Just below the ribcage: **"I am my own teacher."**
3. Heart: **"I am one with my spirit."**
4. Left shoulder/neck: **"I do not condemn myself."**
5. Forehead: **"I forgive everyone, including myself."**
6. Crown, rotating clockwise: **"Let me experience the state of meditation."**

Then hold the hand about 6 inches above the head and feel for the breeze, and sit 5–10 minutes in silence.

### 4.3 Supporting techniques
- **Raising the Kundalini.** The left hand rises in front of the body from the lower abdomen while the right hand circles it clockwise. Above the head, tie a knot. Repeat 3 times: 1 knot, then 2, then 3.
- **Bandhan (shield of protection).** Left palm up on the lap. The right hand arcs from the left hip over the head to the right hip and back, about 2 inches from the body. **7 times.** Done before and after meditation.
- **Foot-soak.** Every evening, ideally before bed. Lukewarm water to the ankles, a handful of salt, a candle in front, palms up, **eyes open**. Ask: "Mother Kundalini, please absorb all the imbalance from my chakras and channels into salt water." Up to 10 minutes, then rinse, flush, and meditate. "Salt water = ocean = guru principle; water + earth."
- **Ice pack** on the liver to cool the right side. **Candle** flame for the left side. **Clockwise rotation** of the right hand in front of a chakra (the natural spin).
- **Elements:** earth (sitting on the ground, incense), water (foot-soak), fire (candle), air (breath), ether (sky gazing, raising the hand).
- **A daily routine** at home, in front of Shri Mataji's photo with a candle, plus weekly collective meditation.
- **Music:** Indian classical ragas by time of day (Bhairav at dawn, Yaman in the evening, Bageshri at night, Malkauns), bhajans and qawwali. Shri Mataji emphasised that classical music "balances" the subtle system. The USA site lists ragas, flute, santoor, cello and Om tracks.

### 4.4 Learning journey (how they actually teach)
- **First experience:** a 10-minute "Experience it now" self-realization.
- **7-day video course:** self-realization → channels → lower three chakras → foot-soaking → elements → chakras 4–5 → chakras 6–7.
- **Free 21-day online course:** runs several times a year (latest **22 Sep – 13 Oct 2026**, 8:45 pm ET), followed by **"Going Deeper"** sessions (63+ days). Each day pairs a theme with a Shri Mataji quote, e.g. "Day 1 – Experience the silence within", "Day 8 – Cooling down the right side with ice", "Day 9 – Clearing the left channel".
- **Weekly local classes:** free, about 1 hour, run by volunteers, in libraries and centres, plus Zoom.
- **Kids:** Saturday Zoom sessions with music, games and crafts, grouped by grade.
- **Event hooks:**
  - International Day of Yoga (21 June)
  - The "Rose de Chine" film screenings
  - "We Are One" live music meditation tours
  - Gandhi Jayanti (2 Oct): live sessions in 17 languages
- **Downloadable handouts:** chakra guides, A1 posters, a meditation worksheet (before/after hand charts, a daily log), and a **Meditation Manual**. Raleway is the recommended font for their slides.

## 7. Research and evidence
- **Manocha et al. (2011)**, *Evidence-Based Complementary and Alternative Medicine*. An RCT with 178 full-time workers over 8 weeks, comparing "mental silence" meditation with a relaxation control and a wait-list. The meditation group showed significant reductions in work stress (p = .026) and depressed mood (p = .019) versus both controls.
- **Manocha, Black & Wilson (2012).** A survey of 343 long-term Australian practitioners. Better SF-36 scores on 6 of 8 subscales than national norms. Mental health correlated with how often people experienced mental silence. The study is cross-sectional, so it cannot show causation.
- **Hernández, Rubia et al. (2016, 2018).** Long-term practitioners have larger grey matter and different functional connectivity in the anterior cingulate and other regions, linked to mental silence.
- Earlier small studies on **asthma, ADHD and epilepsy** (Australia and India) exist, but these are small samples.

**Use evidence carefully.** Phrase it as "research suggests it may help with stress and mood". **Avoid the disease claims** that appear in the USA handouts (e.g. linking chakra imbalance to cancer, diabetes or heart attack). They aren't scientifically supported and carry legal and ethical risk on a public site.

## 8. Controversy and criticism (know it; don't be blindsided)
- **Cult allegations:**
  - 1995 Guardian "A-Z of cults"
  - 2001 Independent ex-member accounts (pressure to cut family ties)
  - Belgium's IACSSO advisory (2005), upheld on appeal in 2011
  - Monitoring listed by Belgian state security (2013)
  - An Austrian ministry report
  - Judith Coney's academic study *Sahaja Yoga* (1999)
- **Recurring themes in the criticism:**
  - Arranged marriages
  - Children educated away from their parents
  - Gender roles (men hold most authority)
  - Donations for international pujas
  - The founder treated as unquestionable
  - Beliefs disclosed to newcomers in stages
- **Defenders' view:**
  - Teaching is free and there is no aggressive recruitment (2001 Evening Standard).
  - Some see the movement as unfairly targeted by anti-cult groups.
  - There is internal debate among practitioners about how openly to state the founder's divinity.
- **Organisation:**
  - World Council for the Advancement of Sahaja Yoga (WCASY), 2003 onward.
  - **Vishwa Nirmala Dharma** (registered as a religion in some countries).
  - National trusts: Life Eternal Trust (UK and others), Sahaja Yoga USA.
  - The Shri Mataji Foundation (biography site and archive).

**Implication for the website:** be transparent and welcoming. Never use bait-and-switch framing. State clearly that it's free, who founded it, and what practitioners believe. Make no medical claims, and don't use dark-pattern onboarding.

## 9. What this means for the website / web app

### 9.1 Tone and positioning decisions needed (from the client/collective)
1. **Public versus devotional.** The official public sites call Shri Mataji a founder and humanitarian and give no deity names. The devotional framing (deities, pujas, divinity) goes on internal sites. We need to decide which audience this site serves. *Recommended: public seekers first, with practitioner depth behind a "Go deeper" layer.*
2. **Use of Shri Mataji's photo.** It is central to the practice ("watch the picture on the screen") but polarising for newcomers. *Recommended: present it gracefully in the guided experience, with an explanation.*
3. **Which affirmation set** for the guided realization: the 9-step "Mother…" version or the secular 6-step USA version.
4. **Official affiliation.** Is this built with or for a national Sahaja Yoga body? That affects trademark and logo use, the class-finder data, and approval of the wording.

### 9.2 Interaction concepts grounded in the actual teaching
- **"Experience it now"** is the hero ritual, the Sahaja equivalent of Noomo's "click and hold".
  - The 9 hand positions as a guided, voiced, body-mapped sequence.
  - The counter for each affirmation pulses at the chakra's petal number.
  - The Kundalini visually rises chakra by chakra and opens a **rainbow thousand-petal lotus** at the crown.
  - It ends with a **"feel the breeze"** moment: hold your palm over your webcam or phone, a quiet invitation.
- **"Read your hands"** (the killer interactive):
  - An illustrated pair of hands; the user taps where they feel tingling or heat.
  - The app maps it to a chakra and side, explains its qualities, and gives the remedy (ice, candle, foot-soak, affirmation).
  - Save a before/after log, mirroring the official worksheet.
- **The three channels as a scroll mechanic:**
  - Left = past (cool moon tones, memory, emotion); right = future (warm sun tones, planning).
  - Scrolling or cursor movement makes the attention "swing", and stillness settles it into the centre: **stop moving and the noise fades**.
  - This literally rewards thoughtless awareness, so the UI calms down when you're still.
- **Chakra explorer:**
  - A spine of 7 lotuses (+ Void), each with its petal count, colour, element, quality, finger and remedy.
  - Element-driven visuals: earth/fire/water/sky/air/ether/light.
- **Elements toolkit:** an interactive foot-soak timer (10 min), candle, ice, and bandhan or raising-Kundalini gesture tutorials (motion-captured or illustrated loops).
- **21-day journey:** day cards with a theme + quote + guided video, streaks, and calendar add (they already run this format).
- **Class finder:** a world map of free local classes and online sessions. Built on the existing worldwide contacts and Meetup.
- **Shri Mataji timeline:** Chhindwara → Gandhi's ashram → Quit India → Nargol 1970 → Caxton Hall → UN → centenary 2023, told as a scroll story with archival photos (rights needed).
- **Raga clock:** meditation music that changes with the user's time of day (dawn Bhairav → evening Yaman → night Bageshri).
- **"Mental silence" metric:** an opt-in check-in after each meditation ("How many thoughts? Did you feel the breeze?"). A personal, private chart.

### 9.3 Visual identity cues already in use
- **Sahaja Yoga USA wordmark:** "SAHAJA" in red + "YOGA" in orange, with "MEDITATION" letter-spaced below. Bold condensed red headlines in the handouts; Raleway for slides.
- **Shri Mataji Foundation:** deep maroon and burgundy (`#6B1530`-ish), with an elegant serif in the illustrations.
- **Iconography:**
  - Lotus (rainbow for the Sahasrara)
  - The Kundalini as a spiral in a triangle
  - Chakra mandalas with petals: 4-petal square for Mooladhara, 6-petal Star of David for Swadisthan and the Heart, triangle for Nabhi, etc.
  - Candles, flowers, water, the cool breeze, the dandelion seed (used on their guides)
- **Colour canon:** the chakra colours above. They make a natural, meaningful palette system: each section takes its chakra's hue.
