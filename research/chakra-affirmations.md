# Chakra affirmations

Sourced list for the Affirm tab and the Sadhana "Recite" steps. Compiled 2026-10-17.

## How they are said

- Each affirmation can begin with **"Mother"**, **"Kundalini"** or **"Shri Mataji"** [A].
- Centre: "please make me…". Left: "by Your grace, I am…". Right: "verily You are…" [A][D][T].
- No source sets a rigid count. Mantras and affirmations are "used gently and from the heart", not mechanically [A].
- Suggested default count = the chakra's petals. Shri Mataji's guided self-realization follows that pattern (pure knowledge ×6, own master ×10, the Spirit ×12, not guilty ×16) [S]. This is our default, not a rule.
