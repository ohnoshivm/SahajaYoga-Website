## mooladhara-shyam-kalyan.mp3 — Raga Shyam Kalyan (24:20)
- **Performers:** Pandit Vishnu Govind Jog (violin), Pandit Jnan Prakash Ghosh (tabla)
- **Source recording:** "Violin Recital in Raga Desh and Raga Shyam Kalyan", Shri Kashi Sangeet Samaj, Varanasi (collection of Shri Krishna Kumar Rastogi), audio cassette G346. Digitised by the National Cultural Audiovisual Archives (IGNCA).
- **Archive:** https://archive.org/details/dni.ncaa.SKSS-G346-AC
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)
- **Changes:** Extracted the Shyam Kalyan performance from Side B (22:58 to the end; the first part of the side is Raga Desh), added a 2 s fade-in and 4 s fade-out, re-encoded to 160 kbps MP3. The raga was identified by its notes (tivra Ma with shuddha Ma and Ni, no komal Ni) and the change of tuning at 22:58.
